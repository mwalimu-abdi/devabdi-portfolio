Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you use HomRide.

Effective date: November 20, 2026

HomRide respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how HomRide collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our ride-request application serving Mandera Town, Kenya.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, HomRide may collect:

  • Location information: Pickup points, destinations, and location data when you choose to use location-related features.
  • Personal information: Information such as your name and phone number if you provide it when registering or requesting a ride.
  • Ride information: Ride requests, pickup and drop-off locations, fare estimates, and ride details.
  • Device and technical information: Basic technical information necessary for the app to operate, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide ride-request services.
  • Help passengers and drivers coordinate rides.
  • Display pickup and destination locations on maps.
  • Calculate or display route information and fare estimates.
  • Communicate about ride requests.
  • Maintain app security and improve app functionality.

3. Location Information

HomRide uses location-related information to support pickup and destination selection and ride coordination.

Your device may request permission to access your location. You can control location permissions through your device settings. If you deny location access, some location-related features may not function as expected.

4. Third-Party Services

HomRide may use third-party services to provide app functionality, including:

  • Google Maps Platform: Maps, location display, and route-related services.
  • Google services: Infrastructure or technical services required to operate certain app features.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared when necessary to provide ride services, support app functionality, comply with applicable laws, or protect the safety and security of users.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for app operations, service provision, security, or legal obligations.

Users may request deletion of their personal information by contacting us using the contact details provided below.

8. Children's Privacy

HomRide is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization where required by applicable law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
